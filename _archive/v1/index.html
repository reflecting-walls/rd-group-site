How a job runs

Five steps from first call to final walkthrough

  1. Site visit

    We measure, look behind what we can, and talk through what you want and what it will take.

  2. Written quote

    An itemized scope and fixed price within a few days. No surprise line items later.

  3. Permits & schedule

    We pull permits, order materials and give you a start date and a week-by-week plan.

  4. Build

    The same crew every day, a clean site each evening, and updates from Rafal as work moves.

  5. Walkthrough

    We fix the punch list together, pass final inspection and hand over warranty paperwork.

FAQ

Common questions

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. RD Group carries general liability and workers' compensation insurance, and we're happy to send certificates before you sign anything.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. The site visit and written quote are free. Design drawings or engineering, when a project needs them, are quoted separately up front.

Who handles permits and inspections?

We do. We pull the permits, schedule inspections and meet the inspector on site.

How do payments work?

A deposit to schedule the job, progress payments tied to finished milestones, and the final payment after the walkthrough is complete.

Can we stay in the house during the remodel?

Usually, yes. We seal off work areas with dust barriers, protect floors, and clean up at the end of every day.
